Overview

Our project explores multimodal fusion to improve wildfire detection reliability. Instead of relying on a single sensor stream, we merge RGB and thermal predictions using two fusion algorithms and compare performance under the same evaluation conditions.

Why RGB?

Strong visual context (smoke patterns, flame texture), but sensitive to glare and lighting shifts.

Why Thermal?

Reliable heat cues and low-visibility operation, but may confuse hot non-fire objects.

Why Fusion?

Combines complementary strengths and reduces single-modality failure cases.

Method

Training Approach

  • Transfer learning from pretrained YOLO weights
  • Separate training for RGB and thermal modalities
  • Evaluation using Precision, Recall, F1-score, and Accuracy

Data Requirements

  • Paired RGB–thermal samples
  • Consistent labeling for each modality
  • Strict split policy to reduce data leakage

Fusion Algorithms

We compare a tunable weighted late fusion baseline against an agreement-based product fusion method.

Weighted Late Fusion (Baseline)

Pfused = α·Prgb + (1−α)·Pth

Flexible weighting lets us bias toward the more reliable sensor under specific conditions.

Product Fusion (Agreement-Based)

Pfused ∝ Prgb · Pth

Emphasizes cross-modal agreement—high confidence only when both modalities support fire.
