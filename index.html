Overview

We explore multimodal fusion to improve wildfire detection reliability. Instead of relying on one sensor stream, we merge RGB and thermal predictions and compare two fusion strategies under consistent evaluation.

Why RGB?

Strong context (smoke patterns, flame texture), but sensitive to glare and lighting shifts.

Why Thermal?

Reliable heat cues in low visibility, but can confuse hot non-fire objects.

Why Fusion?

Combines complementary strengths and reduces single-modality failure cases.

Method

Training Approach

  • Transfer learning from pretrained YOLO weights
  • Separate models for RGB and thermal modalities
  • Metrics: Precision, Recall, F1-score, Accuracy

Data Requirements

  • Paired RGB–thermal samples
  • Consistent labels for each modality
  • Strict split policy to reduce leakage

Fusion Algorithms

We compare a tunable weighted late fusion baseline against an agreement-based product fusion method.

Weighted Late Fusion (Baseline)

Pfused = α·Prgb + (1−α)·Pth

Bias the final decision toward the modality that performs best under certain conditions.

Product Fusion (Agreement-Based)

Pfused ∝ Prgb · Pth

High confidence only when both modalities agree, reducing one-sensor false alarms.
